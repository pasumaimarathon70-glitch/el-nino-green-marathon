ABOUT THE MARATHON

EL NINO GREEN MARATHON 2026 is a community-focused running event inspired by the environmental impact of El Niño and the growing need for awareness about climate change, rising temperatures, water conservation, and a greener future. Our mission is to connect fitness with environmental responsibility by encouraging people to stay healthy, protect nature, and make sustainable choices.
