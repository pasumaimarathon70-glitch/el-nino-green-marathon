ABOUT THE MARATHON

Run For Environmental Awareness

"EL NINO GREEN MARATHON 2026 is a premier eco-friendly athletic event organized by Pasumai Ingarangal to raise global awareness on environmental conservation, climate change, and sustainable living. Inspired by the severe weather impacts of El Niño, this event blends physical endurance with social purpose. Whether you are an elite athlete, a recreational runner, or a young participant, step onto the track to advocate for tree plantation, water preservation, and a greener future."
