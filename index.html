ABOUT THE EVENT

Why EL NINO Green Marathon?

EL NINO GREEN MARATHON 2026 is an awareness-driven running event organized to bring people together through sport while creating awareness about climate change and the environmental impact associated with El Niño.


Through running, we aim to encourage a healthier lifestyle, environmental responsibility and a stronger connection between our community and nature. Every step is a reminder that a healthier planet starts with responsible action.


Organized by Pasumai Ingraarangal, the event welcomes runners and young participants to run together for a greener future.
