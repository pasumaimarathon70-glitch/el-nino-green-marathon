ABOUT THE EVENT

FITNESS
WITH A
PURPOSE.

EL NINO GREEN MARATHON 2026 is a community-focused running event inspired by the environmental impact of El Niño and the growing need for awareness about climate change, rising temperatures, water conservation and a greener future. Our mission is to connect fitness with environmental responsibility by encouraging people to stay healthy, understand climate challenges, conserve water, protect nature and make sustainable choices. Through this marathon, we aim to bring people of all ages together for fitness, awareness and positive action towards a healthier and greener community.
