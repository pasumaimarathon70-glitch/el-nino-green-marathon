ABOUT THE MARATHON

Run with purpose.

El Niño is a natural climate phenomenon that can influence temperature, rainfall and weather patterns across different parts of the world.


Through EL NINO GREEN MARATHON 2026, we bring runners, families and the community together to create awareness about climate change, environmental responsibility and the importance of a greener future.
